About Mini Donauts

Hey all, I am Jackson, owner of Mini Donauts.

What started as a 10x10 tent grew into a full mini donut food truck built for live event service.

We specialize in the full experience: hot mini donuts made in front of your guests, great smell, and a fun space-themed vibe.

For the 2026 relaunch, we are focused on private events, public markets, and repeat customers across DuPage County and nearby suburbs.
